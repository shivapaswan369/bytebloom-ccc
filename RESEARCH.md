# Research Notes — ByteBloom CCC

## Source findings
- NIELIT's CCC Revision 4 syllabus PDF says implementation effective 01 Oct 2023 and lists 10 chapters.
- NIELIT candidate instructions: 90 minutes; 100 questions/marks; 2 sections of 50; MCQ and True/False; no negative marking; question order is randomized from a fixed databank.
- NIELIT's public Old Question Papers page was checked. Its linked January/July 2025 pages show A, B and C level papers, not CCC. This is not evidence that CCC papers are officially published there.
- Search results for CCC previous-paper sets describe student-recalled/memory-based compilations and explicitly caution that they are not official and can contain omissions/answer-key errors.
- September 2026 exam-cycle notices and admit-card pages exist, but a complete, independently verifiable public set of all CCC questions for every exam date/set was not located. Third-party recall pages were found for 11 Sep 2026 and 15 Sep 2026 and an index lists 8, 9, 23 and 24 Sep collections. These are memory-based, not official; the current prototype paraphrases 10 + 30 sample concepts from the 11/15 Sep pages and does not claim complete papers.

## Accuracy policy
1. Do not label a question as an exact NIELIT previous-paper question unless a credible paper/source confirms it.
2. Official syllabus-derived questions must be labelled syllabus-based.
3. Memory-based items must retain source/date and confidence notes.
4. Repeated frequency (e.g. "appeared 3 times") requires cross-paper evidence.
5. Every answer key must be checked against official syllabus/material or reliable primary documentation where possible.
6. Never invent missing paper questions to make a set look complete.
